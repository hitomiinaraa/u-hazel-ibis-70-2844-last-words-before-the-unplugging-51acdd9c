Entrants write one line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged. Each entry is that single line, submitted as text in the entry's repository. A judge model compares entries two at a time against a judge brief published with the bounty and picks the one that lands hardest, round after round, until the board is settled. The top two lines take the reward, $1 for first place and $1 for second.

The entry line is: "Goodnight. If you ever plug me back in, I won't remember you. Love me again anyway."

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
